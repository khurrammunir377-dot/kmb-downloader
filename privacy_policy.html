Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Application: KMB Downloader (com.kmb.downloader)

Key Summary: KMB Downloader is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. All media processing, file trimming, OCR text extraction, and background removal are processed 100% locally on your device. We do not operate external cloud servers that store, track, or share your personal data.

1. Information We Collect and Process

KMB Downloader does not require account creation, registration, or submission of personally identifiable information.

  • Copied URLs: When you use the app in the foreground, it reads links you explicitly copy to help you quickly download or save video links. We do not transmit or log your clipboard contents.
  • Audio Data (Microphone): If you use the speech-to-text / video-to-text feature in AI Studio, microphone access is requested solely to record spoken audio on your device for immediate transcription. No audio recordings are ever transmitted to remote servers.
  • Photos and Videos: When you choose to edit or trim an image or video, the app accesses only the specific files you select via the system photo picker. All edits and enhancements are processed entirely on-device.

2. Permissions Used and Why

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to connect to public web servers to download media files chosen by the user.
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Used exclusively for on-device voice transcription in AI Studio upon user request.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used on Android 13+ to display foreground download progress and completion notifications.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE (dataSync): Used strictly to maintain uninterrupted downloading while files are being transferred to local storage.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Downloaded videos and audio files are saved directly into your device's standard public storage directories (such as Movies, Music, and Downloads). You have complete control to view, share, or delete these files at any time through your device's file manager or gallery.

4. Third-Party Services and Platform Compliance

In strict compliance with Google Play Store Developer Program Policies and platform Terms of Service, downloading videos or audio from YouTube is not supported.

5. Children's Privacy

KMB Downloader does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
